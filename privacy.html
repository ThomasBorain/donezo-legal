Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Donezo ("we", "our", or "us") is a family chore-tracking app. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Profile data: display name, avatar, and colour you choose during setup — stored on our servers to show your family members your profile.
  • Chore photos: photos you take to verify completed chores are sent to our AI service for analysis and are not stored after verification completes.
  • Chore records: chore titles, completion results, and earnings — stored so parents and children can track progress.
  • Push notification token: used only to send you chore completion alerts. Never shared.
  • Device identifier: a random UUID generated on your device, used in place of an account login. We never collect your name, email, or phone number.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the family chore and earnings features of the app.
  • To verify chore completion photos using AI (Google Gemini). Photos are processed and discarded immediately — not stored or used for training.
  • To send push notifications when chores are completed (iOS only, with your permission).

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising. Data is shared only with:

  • Supabase — our database provider (data storage).
  • Google Gemini — AI used to analyse chore photos (photos only, immediately discarded).
  • Cloudflare — our server infrastructure.
  • Apple — for subscription billing via In-App Purchase.

Children's Privacy

Donezo is designed for family use and may be used by children under 13 under parental supervision. We do not collect more information than necessary and do not allow children to contact strangers. Parents control all family connections and chore assignments.

Data Retention

Your data is stored as long as you use the app. You can delete your data by contacting us at the email below. Chore photos are never retained beyond the moment of AI verification.

Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Row-level security policies ensure each device can only access its own family's data.

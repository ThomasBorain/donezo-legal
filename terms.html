1. The Service

Donezo is a family chore tracking iOS app that requires two iPhones — one for the parent or guardian and one for each child. Parents set up chores, assign monetary values, and invite children via a private link. Children photograph completed chores on their device. AI verifies the photographic evidence and provides a score and feedback. Parents track earnings and mark payments. The subscription is purchased on the parent device.

2. Who Can Use Donezo

Donezo is designed to be used by families. The parent or guardian who sets up the app and purchases the subscription must be at least 18 years of age. Children may use the child device under direct parental supervision. By setting up Donezo and connecting a child device, the parent or guardian confirms they have the authority to consent to the collection and processing of data on behalf of their child as described in our Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

Donezo takes children's privacy seriously. Children using the child device are identified by a randomly generated device identifier only. No email address, password, or other personally identifying information is collected from children. The parent or guardian sets up the child's display name and controls all connection data.

Chore photos taken by children are sent to Google Gemini AI for verification and are immediately discarded after the result is returned. They are never stored on any server. Please review our Privacy Policy for full details of how children's data is handled in compliance with COPPA and POPIA.

4. Subscription and Billing

Donezo requires an active subscription to access all features. The subscription is purchased on the parent device.

  • Monthly plan: $2.99 USD per month — includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers
  • Annual plan: $29.99 USD per year — no free trial, payment is charged immediately at purchase

For the monthly plan, payment is charged to the parent's Apple ID at the end of the 7-day free trial. For the annual plan, payment is charged immediately at the time of purchase. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. To manage or cancel your subscription go to: iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Donezo.
